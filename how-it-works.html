The idea, plainly

Find a factory, send a brief, and see the work through to delivery.

Brand owners come to find manufacturers. Manufacturers come to be found, and to fill their lines. MakerToBrand holds the order in between, from the first quote to the packed carton.

For brand owners

Find, brief, order.

Search manufacturers by city and craft. Buy stock in a cart, or post a production brief and compare quotes.

For manufacturers

A page, a shop, a pipeline.

Your profile is a mini website — photographs, capacity, minimums, certifications. Quote on briefs, take orders, and follow the work through to delivery.

The steps

From the first search to the finished goods.

The same path whether you are buying ready-made stock or asking a factory to make to your brief. Payments are made on the site and held until the goods arrive. This demo never takes a card number and never charges anyone.

01

Find a factory

Search by city, craft and minimum order. Open a profile, look at past work, and start a conversation.

02

Send a brief, or order stock

Buy ready-made from 100 pieces, or post a custom brief. Optional design and tech-sheet support can be added before the brief goes out.

03

Compare quotes

Factories reply with a unit price, a lead time and a note. The brand accepts one and the order is placed on the site.

04

QC checks

A QC Master and merchandisers check fabric, the pattern sheet, measurements and finishing on the brand’s behalf.

05

Delivery

Both sides follow the same tracking. When the brand confirms receipt, payment is released to the factory.

Quality control

A QC team that works for the brand, not the factory.

Every brand membership includes MakerToBrand’s own quality team: a QC Master and QC Merchandisers. They check the fabric, the pattern sheet, the measurements and the finishing, then leave a photo report on the order.

Before cutting

Fabric is approved for GSM, hand and shade, and the pattern and size set are measured against the spec sheet.

On the line

A merchandiser inspects stitching and measurements during production, so problems are caught before the goods are packed.

Before dispatch

A final AQL inspection is done, and the report — with photographs — is attached to the order both sides can open.

Trade safely on MakerToBrand

A fair-use rule, in plain language.

At sign-up, both brands and manufacturers agree to keep their deals on MakerToBrand. That is how payment protection, verified reviews and the extras stay available to them.

Contacts stay locked at first

Phone, email and WhatsApp on a profile unlock after the first paid order between those two parties. Messages mask numbers, email addresses and links shared before that.

Reviews only for on-site orders

A review can be left only after an order paid on the site, and it is labelled “Verified order on MakerToBrand.”

Extras stay on the site

GST invoices, tracking, QC by MakerToBrand, shipping and one-click re-orders come only with on-site payments.

If someone tries to move a deal off the platform, an admin can warn them, remove a verified badge, or block the account. You can try those actions in the admin view.
