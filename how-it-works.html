The idea, plainly

A marketplace with two doors, and one safe till.

Brand owners come to find factories and to place orders. Manufacturers come to be found, and to fill their lines. MakerToBrand holds the money in between.

For brand owners

Find, brief, order.

Search manufacturers by city and craft. Buy stock in a cart, or post a production brief and compare quotes.

For manufacturers

A page, a shop, a pipeline.

Your profile is a mini website — photographs, capacity, minimums, certifications. Quote on briefs, take orders, watch earnings land when goods are delivered.

Payments

Held securely until delivery.

On ready-made orders, the brand pays the full wholesale amount at checkout. On custom work, a deposit (typically 30–50%) starts production, and the balance is collected on delivery.

The intended payments partner is Razorpay. In a live product, funds would sit in a controlled account and move only when the order status changes. This demo never takes a card number and never charges anyone.

This holding of funds, and the factory’s guaranteed payout, apply only to orders paid on MakerToBrand. A private deal has no such protection. Brands pay a ₹2,500 monthly subscription and never a fee at checkout. Manufacturers and wholesalers pay a membership, plus a 3% commission taken from their payout — or 0% on the Premium plan.

Trade safely on MakerToBrand

A fair-use rule, in plain language.

At sign-up, both brands and manufacturers agree to keep their deals on MakerToBrand. That is how payment protection, verified reviews and the extras stay available to them.

Contacts stay locked at first

Phone, email and WhatsApp on a profile unlock after the first paid order between those two parties. Messages mask numbers, email addresses and links shared before that.

Reviews only for on-site orders

A review can be left only after an order paid on the site, and it is labelled “Verified order on MakerToBrand.”

Extras stay on the site

GST invoices, tracking, optional quality inspection, shipping and one-click re-orders come only with on-site payments.

If someone tries to move a deal off the platform, an admin can warn them, remove a verified badge, or block the account. You can try those actions in the admin view.
