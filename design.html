Inside the tech sheet

Everything a factory needs to cut and stitch correctly.

Measurements and spec

A size set and measurement chart, with tolerances, so the sample and the bulk order match what the brand approved.

Pattern details

Construction of the pattern, seam allowances, grading notes and any special blocks the floor should follow.

Fabric, trims and notes

Approved fabric and GSM, trims and labels, and construction notes for stitching, finishing and packing.

How to add it

Tick it when you post a custom brief.

On the custom production form, choose design only, a tech sheet only, or both. The request stays with the brief so the factory works from a clear pack. This is a paid add-on for brands; sample prices are shown until vendor rates are confirmed.
