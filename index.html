Payments

The money stays safe until the goods arrive.

Brands pay the product price through the site. MakerToBrand holds the amount — with Razorpay as the intended payments partner — and releases it when the order is delivered. This protection, and the factory’s guaranteed payout, apply only to orders paid on MakerToBrand. On custom work, a deposit starts production; the balance is paid on delivery.

Why stay on MakerToBrand

The safest deal is the one that stays on the site.

Payment protection, verified reviews and on-site extras apply only to deals that stay on MakerToBrand. That is what makes staying the better choice.

01

Payment protection, only here.

Money is held until delivery, and the factory is guaranteed payment, only on orders paid through MakerToBrand. A private deal has no such net.

02

Contacts unlock after the first order.

Phone, email and WhatsApp stay hidden until a brand pays for the first order on the site. Chat automatically masks numbers and links shared too early.

03

Reviews you can trust.

Only orders paid on MakerToBrand can be reviewed, and every review is labelled “Verified order on MakerToBrand.”

04

One membership, for both sides.

Brands, manufacturers and wholesalers all pay a monthly membership to use the marketplace.

05

Useful extras, on-site only.

GST invoices, order tracking, QC by MakerToBrand, shipping and one-click re-orders come only with payments made through the site.

06

A fair-use rule, quietly enforced.

Both sides agree at sign-up to keep deals on the platform. Moving a deal off-site can mean a warning, a lost verified badge, or a blocked account.
