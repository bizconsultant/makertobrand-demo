How the platform earns

A small cut of every order, and plans for those who want more.

Memberships unlock a verified badge, a higher place in search, and more listings. On top of that, commission falls as the same brand and factory keep trading on the site. Prices in Indian rupees.

Loyalty commission

Stay, and the cut gets smaller.

Repeat orders between the same brand and manufacturer cost less in commission. That is a reason to keep the next order on MakerToBrand, rather than taking it private.
