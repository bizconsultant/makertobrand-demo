How the platform earns

Whoever earns from the marketplace, pays a little to be here.

Brands pay a single subscription and never a fee at checkout. Manufacturers and wholesalers pay a membership, plus a 3% commission on each sale — or none, on Premium. Prices in Indian rupees.

For brands

One plan. No commission, ever.

Full access to manufacturer search, production briefs, quotes and payments held until delivery. You pay only the product price at checkout.

For manufacturers & wholesalers

Get found, and keep more of each sale as you grow.

Memberships unlock a verified badge, a higher place in search, and more listings. Pro and Business pay a 3% commission on each sale, taken from the payout. Premium pays none.

Commission is taken from the seller’s payout when a payment is released. Memberships can be started from sign-up — here, choosing a plan simply shows the confirmation. Nothing is charged in this demo.
